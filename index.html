1 Overview & Local Architecture

Whiteboard Studio Pro ("the App", "we", "our") is designed as a privacy-focused interactive digital whiteboard, classroom presentation tool, and PDF annotation software.

Our foundational engineering principle is Local-First Processing. When you draw diagrams, solve geometry problems, or mark up study materials, all data remains stored in your device's isolated application storage sandbox. We do not transmit, harvest, sell, or sync your private whiteboard sketches to any remote server or third-party cloud.

✔ Offline Guarantee: You can use Whiteboard Studio Pro completely offline (Airplane Mode) with zero degradation of drawing, saving, geometry tools, and PDF annotation features.

2 Device Permissions & Data Access

The App requests only the strictly minimal Android operating system permissions necessary to deliver core features:

3 Advertising & Google AdMob

To keep Whiteboard Studio Pro freely accessible, the App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob when connected to the internet.

Google AdMob may collect and process telemetry data including:

  • Google Advertising ID (AD_ID) for ad measurement and frequency capping.
  • Coarse device information (device model, operating system version, and general IP address region).
  • Ad interaction metrics (impressions, clicks, and rewarded video completion).

All ad serving complies with the Google Advertising Policy & Terms. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in your phone's Android Settings ➡️ Google ➡️ Ads.

ℹ️ Ad Frequency Safety: Automatic full-screen interstitial ads strictly observe a minimum 3-minute cooldown timer, ensuring an uninterrupted educational experience.

4 AI Features & Google Gemini API

Whiteboard Studio Pro includes optional smart teaching capabilities (AI Math Explainer, Handwriting OCR, and Practice Exam Generator) powered by the Google Gemini API.

  • Bring Your Own Key (BYOK): Users and teachers can enter their personal Google Gemini API key.
  • Encrypted On-Device Storage: Your Gemini API key is stored locally in encrypted Hive storage on your physical device.
  • No Model Training: Your private questions or formulas are sent directly to the official Google Gemini endpoint over HTTPS and are not used by us to train AI models.

5 Children’s Privacy (COPPA & FERPA)

Whiteboard Studio Pro is widely utilized in classrooms and homes by teachers, parents, and students of all age groups.

  • We do not knowingly collect, harvest, or request personal information from children under 13 years of age.
  • The App does not require account creation, social logins, real names, or chat rooms.
  • In classroom and student modes, ads are filtered to family-safe categories compliant with Google Play Families Policy.

6 User Rights, Data Control & Deletion

Under global privacy regulations (including GDPR, CCPA, and Indian IT Rules), you have total ownership and control over your data:

  • Individual Deletion: Tap the 3-dots icon on any saved sketch card on the home screen to instantly delete that sketch and its thumbnail.
  • One-Tap Complete Erase: Open the in-app Privacy & Data Safety dialog and tap "Delete All Saved Sketches & Data" to permanently purge all cached notes and local settings.
  • Uninstall Erase: Uninstalling the App immediately removes all sandboxed files from your Android device.

7 Security Measures

We implement rigorous cryptographic and operating system safeguards to protect user experience:

  • Anti-tampering verification to prevent modified or malware-injected clones from running.
  • All network communications (AdMob, AI requests, P2P sync) utilize industry-standard TLS 1.3 encryption.
