Privacy Policy — Whiteboard Studio Pro

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Overview & Local-First Data Storage

Whiteboard Studio Pro ("the App") is an interactive digital whiteboard, PDF/image annotation tool, and classroom geometry studio. All sketches, drawings, notes, and imported PDF/image annotations created in the App are stored 100% locally on your device. We do not operate any cloud database that collects or stores your personal sketches or documents.

2. User Files (Photos, Camera & PDF Documents)

  • Camera & Photo Gallery: Accessed only when you explicitly choose "Import Image" to place a photo onto the whiteboard canvas.
  • PDF Document Picker: Accessed only when you explicitly choose "Import PDF" to render and annotate PDF pages on the whiteboard.
  • Your imported files remain on your device and are never uploaded to external servers.

3. Advertising & Google AdMob (AD_ID)

When used with an active internet connection, the App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob (including App Open ads, occasional New-Sketch interstitial ads, and user-initiated Rewarded/Interstitial ads to unlock Pro tools for the active sketch). Google AdMob may collect and use your device's Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID), coarse device information, and IP address for ad delivery, frequency capping, and fraud prevention in accordance with Google's Privacy & Terms.

4. Data Deletion & User Control

Because all sketches and settings are stored locally on your device, you can delete individual sketches at any time or tap "Delete All Saved Sketches & Data" inside the App's Privacy Policy & Data Safety dialog (or clear app storage in Android Settings) to permanently erase all local data.

5. Children's Privacy & Classroom Use

The App does not require user registration, accounts, phone numbers, or email addresses. Offline mode operates without loading any advertisements.
